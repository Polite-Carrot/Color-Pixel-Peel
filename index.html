    How to play

    1. Every level is a picture made of colored tiles.
    2. The blocks below show a color and a number. Play one into the panel and it takes that many tiles of that color off the picture.
    3. A tile can only be taken if it is reachable — touching a gap, or on the edge of the picture. Tiles walled in by others have to be uncovered first.
    4. A block with nothing to take waits in its slot until tiles of its color are uncovered, and the number on it says how many it is still owed.
    5. Tie up every slot with blocks that cannot move and there is no way on. Undo a play or start the picture again.
    6. Clear every tile to finish the picture.
